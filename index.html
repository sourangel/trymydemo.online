Good to Know

Common questions

How often should I service my AC in Arizona?
At least once a year — ideally in early spring, before the first heat wave. Arizona’s dust and extreme run-times wear systems faster than most climates, so an annual tune-up is the single best way to avoid a mid-summer breakdown.
My AC is still cooling — do I really need a tune-up?
Yes. A system can still run while quietly losing efficiency or heading toward a failure. A tune-up catches low refrigerant, worn capacitors, and dirty coils early — while they’re a cheap fix instead of an emergency.
How long does a new AC installation take?
Most residential changeouts are done in a single day. We’ll give you a firm timeline and a flat quote before we start, and we always clean up before we leave.
Do you offer emergency service on weekends?
We do. We answer calls nights and weekends and prioritize no-cool emergencies — because in a Phoenix summer, a broken AC isn’t something that can wait until Monday.
